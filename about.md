  <h1>About How to Learn</h1>
  <div class="prose">

<p>Hi, I'm <strong>Aashu</strong>. I'm an engineer by education, I have some knowledge of technology, and above all I'm a curious person who likes figuring out how things work.</p>

<p>I started <strong>How to Learn</strong> because most tutorials either skip the basics or bury you in theory. While learning new things myself, from engineering software and electronics to languages and exam preparation, I kept running into the same problem: it was hard to find one clear, honest guide that said what to do first, what to ignore, and how long it really takes.</p>

<h2>What you will find here</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Step-by-step guides</strong> that you can follow in one sitting.</li>
  <li><strong>Realistic timelines</strong> and honest answers, including when something is hard.</li>
  <li><strong>Practical examples</strong> from things I have actually tried.</li>
</ul>

<h2>How I write</h2>
<p>I'm still learning most of these topics as I write about them, and that is the point. I research each guide, try the steps where I can, and publish what worked in simple language. I update posts when software, prices or best practices change. You can read more in my <a href="{{ '/editorial-policy/' | relative_url }}">editorial policy</a>.</p>

<h2>Get in touch</h2>
<p>Found a mistake, or want a guide on a topic? Please <a href="{{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}">contact me</a>. I read every message.</p>

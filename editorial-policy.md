  <h1>Editorial Policy</h1>
  <div class="prose">

<h2>How guides are made</h2>
<p>Each guide is written by the site author, <strong>Aashu</strong>. Topics are chosen because readers search for them or because I have learned the skill myself. I check steps, commands and prices against official documentation or by trying them where possible.</p>

<h2>Accuracy and updates</h2>
<p>If a guide has an error or becomes outdated, I fix it and update the date. To report a problem, <a href="{{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}">contact me</a>.</p>

<h2>Independence</h2>
<p>Recommendations are based on what is useful for learners. If ads or affiliate links are ever present, they will not decide what is written.</p>

<h2>Use of tools</h2>
<p>I may use software tools such as spell checkers or AI assistants for research or editing help. All content is reviewed and approved by me before publishing.</p>

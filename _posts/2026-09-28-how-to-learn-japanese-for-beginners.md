Japanese looks scary at first, but a clear order makes it manageable.

## Start with hiragana and katakana

These two alphabets have 46 characters each. Most people can learn them in two weeks using flashcards.

## Learn words in context

Pick 10 useful words a day and use each one in a short sentence. Say it out loud.

## Speak early

Find a language partner or record yourself. Mistakes are part of the process.

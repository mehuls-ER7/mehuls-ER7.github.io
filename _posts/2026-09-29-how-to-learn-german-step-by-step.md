German is very regular once you know the rules.

## Week 1-2: Sounds and greetings

Learn the alphabet, pronunciation and 30 everyday phrases.

## Week 3-6: Core grammar

Articles (der, die, das), the present tense and basic word order. Do not try to memorise every case at once.

## Every day: Listen

Fifteen minutes of slow German podcasts trains your ear faster than any textbook.

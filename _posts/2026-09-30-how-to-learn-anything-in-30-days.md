You can get surprisingly good at almost any skill in 30 days if you follow a plan.

## 1. Define a clear target

Not "learn guitar" but "play three songs with clean chords".

## 2. Break it into parts

List the 5 smallest pieces of the skill. Practice the weakest piece first.

## 3. Practice in short daily sessions

Twenty to forty minutes, every day, with full focus and no phone.

## 4. Test yourself every week

Teach it to a friend or record yourself. Gaps show up fast.
